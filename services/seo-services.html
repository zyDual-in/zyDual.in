What SEO Is

SEO is the process of improving how search engines index, rank, and display your website. It includes on-site elements, technical health, and external signals that influence search authority.

Local SEO

Local SEO helps businesses appear in map searches, local pack results, and neighborhood queries by optimizing business profiles, reviews, local content, and citations.

Technical SEO

Technical SEO focuses on crawlability, site performance, schema markup, security, and site architecture to ensure search engines can access and understand your website.
