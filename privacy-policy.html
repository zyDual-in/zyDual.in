1. Introduction

Zydual respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information collected through our website, contact forms, lead-generation forms, communications, and services. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and protection of your information.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal information from you:

  • Full name
  • Business or company name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • WhatsApp number
  • Website and social media information
  • Service requirements and specifications
  • Business requirements and preferences
  • Information submitted through contact and lead generation forms
  • Information voluntarily provided during communication with our team

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us or that is necessary to deliver our services.

3. Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (desktop, tablet, mobile)
  • Operating system
  • IP address
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referral source (how you arrived at our website)
  • General website usage information and patterns

This information helps us improve our website performance, understand user behavior, and optimize the user experience.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Responding to your inquiries and requests
  • Providing quotations and cost estimates
  • Understanding your business requirements and goals
  • Delivering our services, projects, and solutions
  • Providing customer support and project communication
  • Improving our services, website functionality, and user experience
  • Marketing and advertising our services (with your consent)
  • Preventing fraud and unauthorized activity
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations

5. Lead Generation Forms

Zydual may collect information submitted through lead-generation forms on platforms such as Facebook and Instagram. When you submit a lead form, you acknowledge and consent to Zydual contacting you regarding:

  • Your inquiry or service request
  • Requested services or solutions
  • Quotations and pricing information
  • Consultation and discovery calls
  • Your specific business requirements

The information you provide through lead-generation forms will be used to evaluate your needs and determine how Zydual can best assist you.

6. Communications

Zydual may contact you using the contact information you provide through:

  • Phone calls
  • WhatsApp messages
  • Email communications
  • Other contact methods you authorize

We will communicate with you only for purposes related to your inquiry, requested services, project updates, support, and relevant business information.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Zydual may use cookies, analytics tools, and similar technologies to:

  • Track website performance and user interactions
  • Analyze traffic patterns and usage statistics
  • Measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns
  • Enhance security and prevent fraud
  • Improve overall user experience and website functionality

These technologies allow us to understand how visitors use our website and to optimize our services accordingly. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

8. Third-Party Services

Zydual may use third-party service providers for various purposes, including:

  • Web hosting and infrastructure management
  • Analytics and traffic analysis tools
  • Advertising platforms and marketing services
  • Email and communication services
  • Automation and workflow management tools
  • Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems
  • Payment processing and billing
  • Software and development platforms

These service providers are contractually obligated to use your information only as necessary to provide services to Zydual and to maintain the confidentiality and security of your information.

9. AI and Automation Services

Zydual provides AI automation and technology services. When you use our services or provide information that is processed through third-party AI, automation, communication, hosting, or software platforms, your information may be subject to those providers' privacy policies and terms of service.

We ensure that all third-party platforms we integrate with meet high standards for data security and privacy protection. However, you acknowledge that data processed through external platforms may be subject to those platforms' terms and policies in addition to this Privacy Policy.

10. Data Security

Zydual implements reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest
  • Secure server infrastructure and access controls
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Restricted access to personal information on a need-to-know basis

Important: While we implement industry-standard security practices, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security. We cannot guarantee the complete security of your information, and you use our services at your own risk.

11. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as it is reasonably necessary for:

  • Delivering our services and completing projects
  • Maintaining accurate business records
  • Resolving disputes or addressing claims
  • Implementing security measures and fraud prevention
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations

After these purposes are fulfilled, we will delete or anonymize your information in accordance with applicable law, unless retention is required for legal reasons.

12. Sharing of Information

Zydual does not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share your information when reasonably necessary with:

  • Trusted service providers and technology partners who assist us in delivering services
  • Advertising platforms and marketing partners (for marketing purposes with your consent)
  • Professional advisers, consultants, and auditors
  • Government authorities, law enforcement, or regulatory bodies when required by applicable law or legal process

Any third parties with whom we share information are required to maintain the confidentiality and security of your personal information and use it only as necessary to provide services on our behalf.

13. User Rights

Depending on applicable laws and regulations in your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction or update of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Request information about how your personal information is being processed
  • Object to the processing of your information for certain purposes
  • Request restriction of information processing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the contact information provided in Section 17 of this Privacy Policy.

14. Children's Privacy

Zydual's website and services are intended for businesses, professionals, and general users and are not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child under 13 has provided information to Zydual, please contact us immediately at the contact information provided in Section 17.

Zydual's website may contain links to third-party websites, including service providers, portfolio projects, and other external resources. Zydual is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of these external websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party websites before providing your personal information. This Privacy Policy applies only to information collected through Zydual's website and services, not to third-party sites.

16. Changes to Privacy Policy

Zydual may update this Privacy Policy at any time when:

  • Our business practices or services change
  • We implement new technology or tools

  • We enhance our privacy and security practices

We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the bottom of this page or by sending you an email notification. Continued use of our website and services after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

Please review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
